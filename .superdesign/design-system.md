# Parkwise — Technical Minimalist design system
New mosaic design-system landing concept on the selected Superdesign canvas. Product: browser-based educational parking and operating-systems lab, not a commercial live parking platform. Audience students and faculty. Preserve honest educational copy; no fabricated customer endorsements or operating statistics.

## Visual direction (user specification, authoritative)
Paper #F7F7F5 background; Forest #1A3C2B brand; Grid #3A3A38 hairlines at 20% opacity. Coral #FF8C69, Mint #9EFFBF, Gold #F4D35E accents. Flat 2D, no shadows, no gradients, radius 0 or at most 2px. Full-page seamless SVG mosaic of interlocking rectangles: large squares, horizontal strips and vertical blocks, paper fills, 0.5px grid strokes at .3 opacity. High negative space and precision.
Space Grotesk bold tight headings 64–96px line-height .9 desktop, responsive 40–56px mobile. General Sans body. JetBrains Mono labels 10–12px .1em uppercase; subtext 14px uppercase with left hairline. Load fonts through public CSS/font URLs with fallback sans-serif/monospace.

## Motion / responsiveness / access
Snappy 160–240ms standard ease-out opacity and color interactions, subtle section entrance, optional smooth scrolling; no3D, WebGL, depth, gradients or shadow. Respect reduced motion. Keyboard navigation, visible focus, semantic headings and labels. Mobile one-column hero and bento, nav compact menu with real toggling, no horizontal overflow. Index links scroll to real section anchors; buttons have meaningful preview actions.
